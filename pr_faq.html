FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answer to common questions about our programs

What is PR Scholars?
PR Scholars Edutech LLP is an institute that integrates academic excellence with defence career preparation, offering structured programs in Mathematics and specialized coaching for Armed Forces entrance exams.
Who founded PR Scholars?
It was founded by Colonel Ramu Ranganathan (Retired), a decorated Indian Army veteran and mathematician with over 30 years of teaching experience and over 27 years of military service and corporate leadership experience. He is an alumnus of Delhi University, Indian Military Academy, and School of Artillery, with MSc, MBA and BSc (Hons) Mathematics degrees and extensive military leadership experience.
What makes PR Scholars unique?
The institute uniquely blends school curriculum coaching with defence exam preparation, focusing on both academic success and leadership development.
Which exams does PR Scholars prepare students for?
Students are trained for Board exams (CBSE, ICSE and State Boards) and engineering, medical, law, and management entrance exams as well as defence services’ entrance exams like NDA, CDS, AFCAT, INET.
Does PR Scholars provide SSB Interview coaching?
Yes, specialized coaching is offered for the Services Selection Board (SSB) Interview, including interview preparation, personality development and leadership training.
What subjects are taught at PR Scholars?
Mathematics is the core focus, but modules also cover reasoning, analytical skills, and exam specific strategies for school curriculum as well as multiple entrance exams.
Does PR Scholars provide flexibility to students in terms of class timings, location and fee payment?
Yes, there are multiple batches for each curriculum which are different timings, which provides flexibility in terms of class timings. there are multiple batches for each curriculum which are different timings, which provides flexibility in terms of class timings. There are both online as well as offline classes (at select locations). PR Scholars also provides flexible terms of fee payment.
Who are the faculty members?
The faculty consists of highly qualified professionals with strong academic backgrounds, teaching experience and expertise in competitive exam preparation.
How does PR Scholars ensure personal attention?
Each student receives customized learning plans, one on one mentorship, doubt clearing sessions and regular performance tracking.
What study material is provided?
Comprehensive material includes board aligned notes, practice sets, mock tests, formula sheets, revision booklets and SSB preparation guides.
Does PR Scholars track student progress?
Yes, at PR Scholars student progress is tracked through periodic tests, detailed progress reports, comparative analysis and performance charts.
Does PR Scholars focus only on academics?
Alongwith striving for Academic excellence, PR Scholars emphasizes holistic development, including discipline, resilience, confidence and leadership qualities.
What teaching methods are used?
Interactive lectures, problem solving drills, real time practice and innovative methods that build conceptual clarity and analytical thinking.
Are mock interviews conducted?
Yes, mock SSB interviews are held to evaluate personality, discipline and analytical skills.
Does PR Scholars prepare students for engineering entrance exams?
Yes, tailored modules are available for engineering entrance exams alongside Board exam preparation.
Is medical entrance exam coaching available?
Yes, PR Scholars provides structured preparation for medical entrance exams.
Does PR Scholars help with law entrance exams?
Yes, specialized modules are available for law entrance exam preparation.
Are management entrance exams covered?
Yes, students can prepare for management entrance exams with dedicated study material and guidance.
What values does PR Scholars instill?
Integrity, perseverance, service to the nation, discipline, and resilience are core values emphasized.
Does PR Scholars offer personality development programs?
Yes, personality development and interview preparation are integral parts of the curriculum.
How are students categorized for learning?
Students are grouped into foundation, intermediate and advanced levels based on performance and learning needs.
What are the institutes under PR Scholars?
PR Scholars operates Padma Maths Pro and Colonel R’s Academy, accessible via the website.
Does PR Scholars provide exam strategies?
Yes, strategy notes include shortcuts, time management techniques and exam oriented problem solving methods.
How does PR Scholars inspire students?
By combining academic excellence with mentorship, discipline and leadership training, students are motivated to push boundaries and rise to their fullest potential.

PR Scholars Edutech LLP pioneers integrated learning, empowering students to achieve academic excellence while preparing for defence careers. With expert guidance, structured programs, and holistic development, we nurture future leaders who excel in classrooms and command respect in uniform — the first institute to unite both aspirations seamlessly.
