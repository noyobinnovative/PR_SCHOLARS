ABOUT PRSCHOLARS

A Legacy of Excellence in
Education

Founded by Colonel Ramu Ranganathan (Retd) is a comprehensive educational ecosystem dedicated to nurturing mathematical excellence and preparing future leaders for the Indian Armed Forces.
With over three decades of experience in education and military service, our founder brings a unique blend of discipline, dedication, and pedagogical expertise to every student's learning journey.

At PR Scholars, we strengthen the foundation of analytical thinking, discipline and resilience. Our institution is dedicated to harnessing the power of Mathematics to sharpen minds and prepare students for success in their Academic domain (both School Curriculum and Competitive Exams) and the most prestigious defense services’ examinations, including NDA, CDS, AFCAT and INET. Beyond Academics, the institute provides specialized coaching for the Services Selection Board (SSB), ensuring that students are equipped with the confidence, leadership qualities and problem-solving skills required to excel in the rigorous selection process. PR Scholars’ unique approach blends Academic excellence with character development, instilling values of integrity, perseverance and service to the nation. With experienced faculty, structured programs and a supportive environment, every student is inspired to push boundaries, embrace challenges and rise to their fullest potential. We don’t just teach Mathematics—we shape leaders for tomorrow, ready to serve with honor and distinction.

MEET OUR FOUNDER

The Visionary Behind PR Scholars

Col Ramu Ranganathan (Retd),
MSc, MBA, BSc (Hons) Mathematics, Founder and Chief Mentor

Colonel Ramu Ranganathan (Retired) is an alumnus of Sri Venkateshwara College, Delhi University, from where he graduated in BSc (Honours) Mathematics. He started his professional journey with Standard Chartered Bank in the late 90’s. He responded to his Higher calling and joined the Indian Army.

He is an Alumni of Indian Military Academy, Dehradun, from where he got commissioned as an Officer into one of the most decorated units of the Indian Artillery. Later, he went on to command the same unit. During his command his unit was nominated to serve in Israel (Golan Heights) and Congo as part of the UN Peace Keeping Force. During the course of his illustrious Military career, he has served in the operationally, physically and mentally demanding High Altitude terrains of Kargil and Dras Sector in Kashmir and the Tawang Sector in Arunachal Pradesh. In Kargil and Dras Sector, the officer had undertaken some live firing actions during the Operation Parakram in 2001, thereby contributing to the unit being awarded the coveted Chief of Army Staff Unit Citation. The unit was also awarded the GOC-in-C Eastern Command Unit Citation in 2016, for its Operational Excellence, when he was the Second-in-Command of the Unit. Colonel Ramu Ranganathan has led some enthralling military operations against the North Eastern Insurgent groups in Assam, Nagaland and Manipur with immense success, while ensuring utmost safety of the troops under his command. The Colonel has also been face to face with Chinese forces, when he served in Tawang Sector in Arunachal Pradesh.

Colonel Ramu Ranganthan is also an alumnus of School of Artillery, from where he completed the coveted Long Gunnery Staff Course and in the process acquired his Post Graduation Degree in MSc (Weapons Technology) from Pune University. He acquired his Masters Degree in Operations management from Narsee Mongee Institute of Management Studies, Mumbai. The Officer is also an alumnus of Indian Institute of Foreign Trade (IIFT), New Delhi, from where he completed a course on International Business Management. In the due course of his Military career, he kept enhancing his knowledge and skills by undertaking various Leadership related courses.

With the honor of serving our nation for more than two decades, Colonel Ramu Ranganathan, stepped back into the Corporate World by joining Wells Fargo in the year 2023 and started guiding the Corporate leaders. His journey has taken him from commanding a large military unit to mentoring global teams and leaders in the Corporate World to establishing PR Scholars for nurturing Academic talents and for mentoring future military leaders. At every stage, his mission has remained constant : To mentor, empower and elevate future leaders.

The Four Pillars of Success

Building tomorrow's leaders through personalized attention, expert guidance, comprehensive resources, and measurable progress

Personal Attention

At PR Scholars, we believe that every student is unique, with individual strengths, challenges and aspirations. Our coaching approach is built on the principle of personalized attention, ensuring that each learner receives the guidance they truly need.

  • • Individual Mentorship: Faculty members closely monitor each student’s progress, offering tailored feedback and strategies for improvement.
  • • Customized Learning Plans: Lessons and practice sessions are adapted to match the pace and learning style of every student.
  • • Doubt-Clearing Sessions: Dedicated time is provided for one-on-one interactions, helping students overcome specific academic hurdles.
  • • Holistic Development: Beyond academics, mentors focus on discipline, confidence-building, and exam temperament.
  • • Continuous Tracking: Regular assessments and performance reviews ensure that students stay on the right path toward their goals.

Expert Faculty

  • • Highly Qualified Professionals: The faculty at PR Scholars comprises subject experts with strong academic backgrounds and years of teaching experience.
  • • Specialized in Board and Competitive Exams: Each faculty member is well-versed in the syllabus, exam patterns and strategies for Board and competitive exams.
  • • Personalized Mentorship: Beyond classroom teaching, they provide one-on-one guidance, doubt-clearing sessions and motivational support to help students stay disciplined and focused.
  • • Innovative Teaching Methods: Use of interactive lectures, problem-solving drills and real-time practice ensures conceptual clarity and analytical thinking.
  • • Result-Oriented Approach: The faculty emphasizes exam-specific preparation, time management and confidence-building, leading to consistent success stories.
  • • Role Models for Students: By blending academic excellence with values of discipline, they inspire students to excel in all spheres.
