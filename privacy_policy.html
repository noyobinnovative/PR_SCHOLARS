PRIVACY POLICY

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email, phone and address provided during registration.
  • Payment Information: Processed securely via ccAvenue. We do not store card details.
  • Usage Data: Browser type, IP address, pages visited, collected via cookies and analytics.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process enrolments and deliver course content.
  • To communicate class updates, schedules and results.
  • To process payments and issue receipts.
  • To improve our website and services.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Payment processors (ccAvenue) for transaction processing.
  • Service providers who assist in operating our platform.
  • Legal authorities when required by law.

Data Security : We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption, secure servers, and access controls. However, no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure.


Cookies : Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience and gather analytics. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect site functionality.


Your Rights :

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
