FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answer to common questions about our programs

What is Colonel R’s Academy?
Colonel R’s Academy is a premier institute dedicated to preparing aspirants for NDA, CDS, AFCAT and INET exams while mentoring them for SSB interviews and instilling Officer-Like Qualities (OLQs).
Who founded the academy?
It was founded by Colonel Ramu Ranganathan (Retired), who brings decades of military experience and leadership to guide young aspirants.
Which exams does the academy prepare students for?
We provide coaching for NDA, CDS, AFCAT, INET and also offer mentorship for SSB interviews.
What subjects are taught at the academy?
We cover Mathematics, Numerical & Reasoning Ability, English, General Knowledge and Current Affairs, tailored to each exam’s syllabus.
Does the academy offer specialized mathematics coaching?
Yes, we integrate PMPro methods to strengthen math foundations aligned with competitive exam requirements.
Are mock tests conducted?
Yes, regular mock tests and practice papers are conducted with detailed performance analysis.
What is adaptive learning at the academy?
Adaptive learning means personalized study plans based on each student’s strengths and weaknesses.
How does the academy prepare students for SSB interviews?
Through psychological testing, group tasks, personal interviews and personality development workshops.
What psychological tests are covered?
We guide students through TAT, WAT, SRT and self-description exercises.
Are group tasks and discussions part of training?
Yes, we simulate GTO tasks, group planning exercises, discussions and lecturettes.
Is personal interview training provided?
Yes, one-on-one sessions help students build confidence, clarity and OLQs.
Does the academy focus on personality development?
Yes, workshops cover communication skills, leadership and stress management.
Is physical fitness training included?
Yes, daily PT, obstacle courses and endurance building are part of the routine.
How is discipline instilled in students?
Structured schedules and military-style routines foster discipline and time management.
Does the academy provide mindset coaching?
Yes, students are trained in resilience, adaptability and decision-making under pressure.
What infrastructure does the academy offer?
We have dedicated study zones, a library, smart classrooms, obstacle course and online learning support.
Are online classes available?
Yes, we offer a hybrid model with live classes, doubt-clearing sessions and recorded content.
Does the academy promote military culture?
Yes, the environment reflects discipline, patriotism and service ethos.
Are guest lectures conducted?
Yes, retired officers deliver lectures and workshops to inspire students.
Does the academy engage with schools and colleges?
Yes, seminars are conducted to raise awareness about defence careers.
Is parent engagement encouraged?
Yes, sessions help families understand the journey and support aspirants.
Does the academy have a social media presence?
Yes, we share motivational content, exam tips and student success stories online.
What makes Colonel R’s Academy different?
Our dual focus on academic excellence and OLQs ensures holistic grooming of future leaders.
Are both boys and girls eligible?
Yes, we provide equal opportunities with tailored guidance for each pathway.
What career benefits do the Armed Forces offer?
A stable salary, pension, perks, adventure, respect and the pride of serving the nation.
